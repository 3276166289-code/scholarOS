# scholarOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

scholarOS is an app that runs inside Anna. This policy covers what the app itself
does; Anna's own data practices are governed by Anna's privacy policy.

## What scholarOS stores

- **Your content.** Projects, papers you upload, extracted text, references,
  notes, captions, and the star map are stored in your own Anna app storage,
  scoped to your Anna account.
- **Nothing else.** There is no separate account, no analytics, no crash
  reporting service, and no advertising identifier.

## What leaves your device

- **Model calls.** When you translate, polish, rewrite, or ask for a review, the
  text you send goes to your Anna model provider. Those calls are made by Anna
  on your behalf and are subject to Anna's terms.
- **Third-party lookups, only when you ask.** If you search a DOI, fetch an
  arXiv entry, read an RSS feed, or connect a reference manager, the app makes
  that request from the tool process on your behalf. The query terms are what
  you typed or the identifiers in the files you uploaded.
- **Nothing else.** Text extraction, PDF page rendering, DOCX export, image
  conversion, PDF merging, citation formatting, and the compliance check run
  locally in a tool process and do not contact any server.

## Credentials

If you connect a reference manager or mail account, the token is stored in your
Anna app storage and used only for the requests you initiate. scholarOS does not
transmit it anywhere else.

## Retention and deletion

Your data lives in your Anna account's app storage. Deleting the app or clearing
its storage removes it. Uploaded files you delete are removed from storage; the
app keeps no copy.

## Children

scholarOS is not directed at children under 13.
