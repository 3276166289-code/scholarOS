## Long description

```
scholarOS is a workbench for the part of research nobody enjoys: turning a folder of PDFs into something you can submit.

Upload a paper and scholarOS reads it — page count, text, figures, and per-page diagnostics that tell you when a page is a scan and needs OCR. Tables and figures come out as images at the resolution you ask for, and every caption starts from a template you can edit instead of a blank box.

References are where it earns its keep. Paste BibTeX or RIS and scholarOS drops duplicates, names the fields that are missing, and formats what is left in APA 7, IEEE, or Nature style. The compliance check is deliberately boring: it counts your figures and tables, flags gaps in numbering, catches in-text citations that point past the end of your reference list, finds numbers glued to units, and lists abbreviations you never defined. It does not grade your science and it does not guess.

Everything computational runs in a local tool process: text extraction, page rendering, DOCX export, image conversion, PDF merging. No upload to a third party, no queue, no wait. Writing, rewriting, and reviewing go through your Anna model, so the words stay yours and the model is the one you already pay for.

Your projects, library, notes, and star map stay in your own Anna storage.
```

222 words.
